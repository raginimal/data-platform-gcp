AI Engineering Golden Path

Framework Overview and Workflow

A reusable, technology-neutral framework: policies define the rules, deterministic CI enforces them, AI agents advise, and humans decide.

Delivery workflow

Every change follows this lifecycle, from work item to production learning. See docs/developer-workflow.md.

Developer Automation Human decision Platform / operations

Framework layers

Each layer has a single responsibility and a clear owner.

Policies

Source-of-truth rules with stable principle IDs under policies/.

Deterministic CI

Scripts and pipelines enforce checks, baseline guard, and artifact promotion.

AI agents and skills

Architecture, security/DevOps, code quality, and AI engineering reviewers. Advisory only.

Human governance

CODEOWNER approval, risk classification, exceptions, and production sign-off.

Infrastructure and config

Overlay-based, cloud-neutral core with per-environment configuration.

Observability and learning

Monitoring and incident feedback flow back into policy.

Who decides what

RoleResponsibility
AI agentRecommends, summarizes evidence, flags risk. Cannot approve, merge, or grant exceptions.
CIDeterministic enforcement of security, quality, policy, and baseline rules.
HumanArchitectural decisions, risk acceptance, exceptions, and production release approval.
